# Chapter authorization and discovery checklist

Before real data or production:

- [ ] Identify an authorized chapter sponsor and verify current leadership and approval delegates.
- [ ] Establish national-versus-chapter system ownership, data authority and required approvals.
- [ ] Confirm the current award cycle, institutional relationships and institution-provided selection process; the app must never select or authorize awards.
- [ ] Identify donor/accounting source of truth and reconciliation ownership.
- [ ] Confirm membership categories, role/assignment access and identity onboarding/offboarding.
- [ ] Confirm current funding-designation and underwriting policies; verify any overhead claims.
- [ ] Approve privacy notice, consent forms, retention/erasure schedule, media rights and institutional/research permissions.
- [ ] Approve branding, original styling and any public release/distribution authority.
- [ ] Identify approved chapter-owned integration accounts and least-privilege scopes; do not use personal accounts by assumption.
- [ ] Approve operating budget, paid-model/media limits, support and security owner.
- [ ] Identify deployment, launch, incident response and rollback authority.
- [ ] Test production identity, RLS, private storage, backups, job recovery and revocation in staging.
- [ ] Obtain explicit approval for every live communication, external write, paid job or release. Credentials alone never enable an integration.
