# Scientific review

Every claim stores exact wording, authorized source, evidence scope, classification (established/preliminary/hypothesis/future_goal), limitations, plain-language version, scene references and reviewer decision. Unsupported superlative/causal phrases are flagged for wording revision. Automated phrase checks are aids, not scientific validation.

Only the designated scientific reviewer can record the dedicated claim-review decision. Claim approval and final-cut scientific gate are separate. Unreviewed claims or unresolved wording flags block readiness. New script, claim, cut or other relevant material changes invalidate final-cut reviews. Draft scene references preserve the source, quote and asset IDs.

No fictional result is attributed to a real scholar. Negative results, incremental progress, uncertainty and team contributions are welcome; no forced breakthrough arc or causal assertion that an award produced an outcome. Generated/illustrative visuals are never documentary research evidence. The demo laboratory and transcript are fictional.
